Who We Are

Wakrie Stores is a Nairobi-based retail shop located at Hirsons Plaza, Kamukunji. We started with a simple idea: that every family, student and sports enthusiast deserves access to quality products at honest prices.

Founded in 2018, we have grown from a small corner stall into a trusted neighbourhood store serving hundreds of customers every month. Our range covers children's toys, school stationery, sports equipment and much more.

Whether you walk through our door or reach us online, our commitment to you is the same — great products, fair prices, and service that keeps you coming back.
